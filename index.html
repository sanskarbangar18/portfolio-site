Automatic Temperature Controlled Fan (Arduino Uno)

Academic project · Tinkercad simulation · VJTI

A simulated embedded system that reads temperature with a TMP36 sensor and switches a DC fan on at 30 °C and above.

Arduino UnoTMP36NPN TransistorDC MotorEmbedded C++Serial Monitor
View project report
Case study

Problem

Cooling fans that run continuously waste energy and make unnecessary noise, while manual switching is inefficient.

Approach

  • Read the TMP36 analog output on pin A0 and converted it to degrees Celsius in code: (voltage - 0.5) x 100.
  • Compared the reading with a 30 °C threshold every second, driving pin 3 HIGH (fan ON) or LOW (fan OFF) through an NPN transistor.
  • Printed live temperature to the Serial Monitor at 9600 baud to observe the behaviour.

Result

Verified in Tinkercad simulation: the fan stayed OFF at 24.78 °C and switched ON at 30.16 °C, with readings matching the expected voltage-to-temperature conversion.
